Contact Cynevor

Start a thoughtful conversation

Cynevor welcomes enquiries from businesses, organisations, potential partners, and anyone interested in the wider direction of the group. Whether your enquiry relates to a digital project, a partnership, or a broader strategic conversation, please get in touch.

Cynevor is currently a founder-led pre-incorporation project. Enquiries are reviewed directly and routed according to relevance and current delivery capacity.

Enquiry pathways

  • Digital products, websites, apps, and platforms
  • Partnership and strategic collaboration
  • Academy and foundation-related conversations
  • General group enquiries

Get in Touch

Direct contact details

The best way to reach Cynevor is by email. We aim to respond to all relevant enquiries within a reasonable timeframe and will always acknowledge receipt.

  • Email: contact@cynevor.com
  • Location: Wales, United Kingdom

For enquiries relating to our services, please include a brief description of your project or requirement to help us respond effectively.

Before you get in touch

  • Review our Services page for digital project details
  • Review our About page for background on the group
  • Include clear context and purpose in your message
  • Use the most relevant pathway for faster routing

Strategic Enquiries

For broader strategic conversations

We welcome thoughtful, considered conversations grounded in shared values and mutual purpose. Please include relevant background in your initial message so we can respond meaningfully.

Strategic conversation areas

  • Partnership and collaboration opportunities
  • Investment or strategic alignment conversations

How an enquiry works

A simple process from first message to next step

1

You share context

You send a clear summary of your enquiry and current priorities.

2

We review fit

We review whether your enquiry aligns with Cynevor's focus and capacity.

3

We arrange an initial conversation

If aligned, we arrange an initial conversation to explore context in more depth.

4

We outline possible next steps

We outline practical options for direction, scope, and collaboration approach.

5

If useful, we define scope and proposal

Where appropriate, we define scope and a proposal for the next phase.
