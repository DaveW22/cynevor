Cookie Policy

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on Cynevor websites and how you can manage your browser preferences.

Last updated: 2 June 2026

At a glance

  • Essential cookies may be used for core website operation
  • Analytics cookies may be used to improve site performance
  • You can control cookies in your browser settings
  • Third-party providers may set cookies where embedded services are used

What Cookies Are

Small files stored by your browser

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when visiting a website. They help websites function effectively, remember preferences, and improve user experience.

How Cookies May Be Used

Operational and performance purposes

We may use cookies to support core site functionality, improve performance, understand aggregate usage trends and maintain security.

Cookie Categories

Essential, analytics and preferences

  • Essential cookies for core website operation and security
  • Analytics cookies to understand overall website usage patterns
  • Preference cookies to remember display or interaction choices

Managing Cookies

Your controls

Most browsers allow you to block, delete or restrict cookies. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality.

You can review browser support documentation for Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge to manage settings.
