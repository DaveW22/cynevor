Cookie Policy

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on Cynevor websites and how you can manage your browser preferences.

Last updated: 2 June 2026

At a glance

  • We currently aim to use only essential cookies or similar technologies
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies are currently configured
  • You can control cookies in your browser settings
  • If non-essential cookies are introduced, this policy will be updated

What Cookies Are

Small files stored by your browser

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when visiting a website. They help websites function effectively, remember preferences, and improve user experience.

Current Use

Essential operation only

As of the last updated date shown above, Cynevor aims to use only essential cookies or similar technologies required for basic website operation, reliability, and security.

Cynevor does not currently use non-essential analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. If non-essential cookies are introduced, this policy will be updated and consent controls will be added where required.

What Is Not Currently Configured

Non-essential tracking

  • No analytics or measurement cookies are currently configured
  • No advertising or remarketing cookies are currently configured
  • No tracking pixels are currently configured
  • No embedded video player cookies are currently configured

Managing Cookies

Your controls

Most browsers allow you to block, delete or restrict cookies. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality.

You can review browser support documentation for Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge to manage settings.

Future Changes

Consent for non-essential cookies

If Cynevor introduces analytics, embedded services, or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy with clear cookie details and consent controls. Non-essential cookies should not run until valid consent has been given, and consent options will include a way to change or withdraw preferences.
